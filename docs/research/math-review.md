## Derivations referenced above

- **Item 5.** Standard normal truncated to [−k, k]: by symmetry the mean is 0; `E[Z²] = 1 − 2kφ(k)/(2Φ(k)−1)` (integrate z²φ(z) by parts over [−k, k] and divide by the retained mass `2Φ(k) − 1`).
- **Item 16.** For normal, cp ≥ 1: `3|a_i|σ_i = |a_i|t_i/cp_i ≤ |a_i|t_i`; and `sqrt(Σ x_i²) ≤ Σ|x_i|` for any reals. Hence RSS 3σ ≤ WC. Either inequality can be broken by cp < 1 or by `3σ_i = √3·t_i` (uniform).
- **Item 18.** Wilson limits at x = 0: centre `(z²/2N)/(1+z²/N)`, half-width `(z²/2N)/(1+z²/N)` → upper `z²/(N+z²)`, lower 0.
- **Item 19.** Var(s_N − s_{N/2}) ≈ Var(s_{N/2}) − Var(s_N) for nested samples (s_N is, to first order, the average of the two half-sample estimates) → σ²(1/N − 1/(2N)).
- **Item 22.** For θ ~ N(0, s²): `E cos θ = e^{−s²/2}`, `E cos²θ = ½(1 + e^{−2s²})`.
- **Item 27.** Uniform disk radius R: `E[x²] = (1/πR²)∫∫ r²cos²φ · r dr dφ = R²/4` → σ = R/2 = T/4.
